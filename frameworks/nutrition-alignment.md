# Framework: Nutrition Alignment System

**Definition:** The physiological and energetic calibration layer ensuring the body can sustain the cognitive and operational load of the work.

**Components:**
- Intake calibration.
- Metabolic pacing.
- Somatic readiness tracking.

**What It Replaces:**
- Caffeine-driven hustle.
- Ignoring physiological limits.

**Application:**
Deployed before high-stakes execution or during recovery phases.
