# Framework: Somatic Sound Architecture

**Definition:** The use of acoustic and resonance patterns to regulate nervous system state and anchor orientation.

**Components:**
- Frequency mapping.
- Resonance pacing.
- Auditory grounding.
- Sonic return protocols.

**What It Replaces:**
- Cognitive-only regulation.
- Mental overriding of physiological signals.

**Application:**
Deployed during calibration sessions, recovery periods, and high-stress transitions.
