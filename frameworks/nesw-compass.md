# Framework: NESW Compass & Dual Orientation Model

**Definition:** A directional navigation system mapping internal (somatic/philosophical) and external (operational/environmental) signals.

**Components:**
- **North:** Internal clarity.
- **East:** External execution.
- **South:** Grounding/Recovery.
- **West:** Integration/Interdependence.

**What It Replaces:**
- Single-axis planning.
- Burnout from over-execution.

**Application:**
Used when founders lose directional clarity or over-index on one domain.
