# Entity Declaration

**Ethan Alan Keen**
*Architect of Recursive Participation*

I do not teach founders to push harder. I teach them to read what is already happening, orient to it, and return as practice. Linear force burns out. Recursive participation sustains.

Ethan operates at the intersection of somatic regulation, philosophical inquiry, operational execution, and sound architecture. His work replaces the "push-through" culture of linear force with calibrated orientation, restoring sustainable agency by teaching founders to read friction as information.
